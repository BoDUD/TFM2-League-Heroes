Image 2 is a TEMPLATE of animation frames of her run (caitlyn run), frames 1-3: in each 512x1024 cell the grey horizontal line is the ground, the short tick is her standing point, the grey bar on the left is her height when standing straight (hat top to soles); the stick figure is the game's skeleton of that frame fitted to her build (blue = her near arm and leg, grey = the far ones, the black circle = where her head with the hat and hair goes and how big it is); the two small BLUE circles are her hands and the RED line is her rifle from the stock's end to the muzzle. Image 3 is the same frames from the game's 3D animation (a chibi model of the same character): copy each frame's POSE from it - how the body leans and twists, where the arms, legs and rifle go. Image 4 is another pixel artist's version of this character doing this kind of move: copy only HOW IT IS ANIMATED (a strong readable silhouette, the body bracing into the move, feet planted), not its costume, colours or proportions.
Create ONE 1536x1024 pixel-art sheet with a genuine transparent background (every pixel fully transparent or fully opaque; if transparency is impossible, a flat pure white background with no glow and no shadow), the SAME three-cell layout as image 2: in each used cell draw the character of image 1 in that frame's pose of image 3, placed and sized by image 2: her soles on the ground line (unless she is in the air), her head where the black circle is and as big, her hands in the blue circles holding the rifle along the red line. Leave unused cells empty.
She and her rifle must be EXACTLY those of image 1 in every frame: the same hat, hair, face with both eyes at the same height, the same colours (image 1's colours only), the same head size, her arms and legs the SAME LENGTH AND THICKNESS as in image 1 (bent, never stretched, shortened, thickened or thinned), the rifle always the same length and thickness. Always the 3/4 view with the face toward the front-right, as image 1, even where the game model turns away.
THE PIXEL GRID: every square the SAME size, 6 px, aligned on one straight grid; the near-black outline of image 1 around the figure; 3-4 flat shades per material, no stray single odd squares, no dithering, no gradients, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no glow.
No muzzle flash, no bullets, no effects, no ground, no shadow, no text. Remove every template mark: no lines, circles, stick figures, numbers or cell borders in the result.
Image 5 is the exact approved colour palette. Use only those swatches for the character (not the palette layout or labels). This sheet is run_1. Exactly 3 occupied cells from left to right, containing frames 1, 2, 3. The canvas has EXACT dimensions 1536 by 1024. Cells span x=0..511, 512..1023, 1024..1535. Standing anchor centres x=256,768,1280. Ground y=874. Standing full height is only 390px (65 pixel cells of 6px): hat top approximately y=484, soles y=874.
