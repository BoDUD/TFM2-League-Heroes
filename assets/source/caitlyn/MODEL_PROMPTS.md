# 凯特琳：按用户选的图在游戏尺寸画造型（给 Codex，第 1 步）

> **这一轮只画造型图（A、B 两版），不画动作。** 用户确认后，下一轮再按它画动作（待机、移动、普攻、爆头、Q 和平使者、W 放夹子、E 射网后跳、R 让子弹飞、死亡）。
> - 照 `caitlyn_source.png`（用户选的 A 图：长枪斜握在身前，枪托在左边腰侧、枪管斜向右上）画：凯特琳的长相、紫色高礼帽、深靛紫长发、白色领巾、棕色短皮外套、紫裙和白褶边、深色紧身裤、棕色长靴、金白色长步枪和站姿。
> - **直接画游戏尺寸**（帽顶到鞋底约 42 格），8 倍放大，每个像素一个 8×8 方块。你画的每一格就是游戏里的一个像素，Claude 只按格子读回，不再缩小（这是卢锡安、薇恩、剑姬成功的做法；先画大再缩会把细节缩成碎点）。
> - 交回时附 `HANDOFF.md`（用了哪段提示词、颜色表、没做到的地方）和 `manifest.json`（尺寸、鞋底行、站位点、不透明区域、眼睛蓝色的中心、颜色数）。文件名 `caitlyn_design_A.png`、`caitlyn_design_B.png`，最好打成一个 zip（放在 outputs 里）。

## 规则（含之前的英雄学到的）

- **游戏尺寸**：帽顶到鞋底约 42 格（高礼帽算在内；本包女英雄算头发约 40 格），真正的低分辨率像素画放大 8 倍：每个像素一个 8×8 的纯色方块，全部对齐同一个 8 px 网格，没有比一格小的东西，没有抗锯齿、模糊、柔光、半透明。
- **干净**：最多 24 种颜色；每种材质 2–3 个平涂色阶（帽子紫、头发靛紫、皮肤、白布、棕皮、裙子紫、紧身裤深蓝紫、金、象牙白、浅青）；大块纯色；不要抖动、渐变、噪点，一块颜色里不要夹单个杂色方块。
- **只有一圈描边**：整个剪影外面一圈 1 格宽的近黑描边；描边里面的边缘和褶皱用那种材质自己最暗的色阶，**不要再画第二圈黑**。白布和象牙白用冷灰色做阴影，里面不要黑线。步枪压在身体前面的地方保留它自己 1 格深色描边，看得出是另一件东西。
- **高礼帽**：约 6 格高（帽冠加帽檐），帽檐比头宽；正面两条金色竖纹，帽底一圈金色帽带，上面一格浅青色宝石。
- **步枪**：从枪托到枪口约 30 格，斜向右上约 35°：中段金色枪身 3 格粗，侧面象牙白护板和一格浅青色能量线；枪身上面立着两个金框圆镜片（各 2×2，中间浅青色）；细长金色枪管 2 格粗，一条不断的直线，枪口 3 格；镂空金色枪托带白色托垫，在左边腰侧。两只戴棕手套的手都握在枪上（近处的手在腰侧握枪柄，远处的手在胸前托住枪身）。
- **脸**（最重要）：A 帽子约 6 格、脸（帽檐下到下巴）至少 8 格；B 帽子约 6 格、脸约 10 格，帽子加头占身高三分之一；两只眼睛都看得见、一样大、在同一行：每只 2 格宽 2 格高（上行一格白色高光 + 一格深色瞳孔，下行蓝色），上面各一行深色睫毛；两眼之间 2 格皮肤，近处眼睛（画面左边，她朝右）前面留一列脸颊；蓝色**只用在眼睛上**（宝石、镜片、能量线用浅得多的青色）；细眉毛用头发最深的靛紫色，贴在眼睛上方、朝鼻子方向稍微往下斜；帽檐和头发都在眉毛上方，中间隔一行皮肤，**绝不盖住眼睛**；一格深玫红色的嘴在脸中线上、眼睛下面两行；脸颊、下巴不要别的深色格（之前的英雄出现过下巴两角的阴影连成歪嘴笑）；头坐在白色领巾上。
- **两版**：A 照原图比例（只把头画大，脸才看得清）；B 按原版英雄的 Q 版比例（帽子加头占身高三分之一，身体和腿短一点），站姿、步枪、颜色不变。
- **脚底线以下什么都不能有**（游戏在脚下画血条）：鞋底最低一行正好在第 99 行方块，下面一格都不能有；枪托在膝盖以上。
- 3/4 正面朝右（照原图），看得到脸和胸口，不画背影。
- 身体在画布中间（照 `caitlyn_size_guide.png` 的位置），枪托伸向左边、枪管斜向右上，所有格子都在画布里。
- 背景透明（做不到用纯品红 `#FF00FF`）；不要网格线、文字、边框、影子。
- 如果模型不肯画有名字的角色，就不提名字，只保留外观描述（下面的提示词里已经没有名字）。

```text
Five attached images. FIRST: the look of this character - copy her face, top hat, long hair, clothes, colors, rifle and her stance from it. SECOND: official heroes of the game Teamfight Manager 2 at 8x (every game pixel an 8x8 block) - match their pixel size, cleanliness and their way of drawing faces and guns. THIRD: heroes of this game's pack at 8x - the quality to reach: crisp small details, no mush. FOURTH: another hero of this pack at game size on the same canvas, 8x - use it ONLY for the size and the ground line. FIFTH: a straight shrink of the FIRST image to game size at 8x - use it ONLY to see what fits at this size and where; it is blurry and broken, do not copy its look.
Task: redraw the FIRST image as clean hand-made pixel art AT GAME SIZE: about 42 pixels from the top of the hat to the soles, true low-resolution pixel art shown enlarged 8x - every pixel one crisp 8x8 square on a single 8-px grid, nothing smaller than one square, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow, no semi-transparency. Keep the big readable shapes of the FIRST image and drop its fine filigree.
The character: a confident sheriff woman standing relaxed as in the FIRST image, weight on one leg, holding a long RIFLE diagonally across her body with both gloved hands: the stock low at her hip on the LEFT, the barrel rising to the UPPER RIGHT at about 35 degrees, the muzzle in front of her above shoulder height. A TALL PURPLE TOP HAT (violet, 2-3 shades) with a flat brim wider than her head, two GOLD vertical stripes on its front and a gold band at its base holding one light-CYAN gem square; very long straight DARK INDIGO-VIOLET hair from under the hat, framing both cheeks and falling behind her shoulders to the waist; fair skin; a white ruffled cravat at the throat with one gold brooch square; a short DARK BROWN leather jacket with gold-trimmed rounded shoulders and dark brown sleeves, brown gloves; a fitted PURPLE bodice and a short flared purple skirt with a gold hem line and a row of white ruffles under it; a brown belt with a gold buckle; dark navy-violet leggings with a brown strap and a small pouch round each thigh, brown knee pads; tall KNEE-HIGH BROWN boots with gold rings and small heels. The RIFLE, about 30 squares long from the butt to the muzzle: a GOLD frame 3 squares thick in the middle with an IVORY-WHITE side panel and a one-square light-CYAN energy line along it, two round gold-rimmed lens rings standing on top (each 2x2 with a light-cyan centre), a long straight GOLD barrel 2 squares thick ending in a 3-square muzzle, and a skeletonised gold stock with a white butt pad at her hip.
Pixel rules (most important): at most 24 colors in total, every material 2-3 flat shades (hat violet, hair indigo, skin, white cloth, brown leather, dress purple, leggings navy, gold, ivory, light cyan); big solid areas; no dithering, no gradients, no noise, no lone square of a different color inside an area. ONE 1-square near-black outline around the whole silhouette only: inside it, edges and folds use the material's own darkest shade - never a second ring of black inside the outline. The white cloth and the ivory are shaded with a cool light grey, never outlined in black inside. The rifle keeps its own 1-square dark outline where it lies over her body, so it reads as a separate object.
Face (most important detail): A: the FIRST image's proportions, only the head bigger than in the FIRST image so the face reads: the top hat about 6 squares tall (crown and brim) and the face (under the brim to the chin) at least 8 squares tall; both eyes visible, the same size and on the same rows: each eye 2 squares wide and 2 tall - top row a white highlight square and a dark pupil square, bottom row BLUE - with a dark lash row over each eye; 2 squares of skin between the eyes and one cheek column before the near eye (left, she faces right); this BLUE is used NOWHERE else (the gems, lenses and energy line are a much lighter cyan); thin brows in the hair's darkest indigo just above the eyes, sloping slightly down toward the nose; the hat brim and the hair stay ABOVE the brows with one row of skin between, never over an eye; one deep rose-red mouth square on the face's middle line two rows under the eyes; no other dark squares on the cheeks or the jaw (a shadow joining the jaw corners to the chin reads as a grin); the head sits on the white cravat.
Pose and place: 3/4 FRONT view facing right as the FIRST image, face and chest visible, never her back. The soles on block row 99 (y 792-799) - the same ground as the FOURTH image - and NOTHING on or below row 100 (the game draws the health bar under the soles): the rifle's butt ends above the knees. Horizontally as the FIFTH image: her body in the middle, the stock reaching to the left and the barrel to the upper right; keep every square inside the canvas.
Layout: one 1024x1024 image per version (a 128x128-square canvas at 8x). Transparent background (if not possible: solid #FF00FF magenta). No grid lines, no text, no border, no shadow.
Before finishing, check: about 42 squares from the top of the hat to the soles; every square 8x8 on one grid; at most 24 colors; both eyes 2x2, level and visible, the blue only in the eyes; the outline one square wide everywhere with no black ring inside it; the barrel one unbroken straight line; nothing below the soles.
```

## Claude 收到后（给 Claude 看）

- 按原稿自己的格子取像素（skill 的 `scripts/regrid.py`：颜色变化的峰值定格子边界，每格取中心 3×3 的中位色），**不缩小**；压色板到 24 色以内，只留一圈描边，蓝色只用在眼睛；手修脸（两眼同高同大、嘴在中线）；右邻同色比例、颜色数和原版英雄比。游戏尺寸预览（和原版英雄、包里英雄比大小，在对战场地色和深色头像卡上各看一遍）给用户挑 A/B。
