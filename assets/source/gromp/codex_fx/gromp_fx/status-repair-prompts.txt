=== q_poison ===
Make ONE PIXEL-ART ANIMATION SHEET, EXACTLY SIX equal portrait cells in ONE horizontal row, no labels, no borders. Total aspect ratio 3:1, each cell aspect 1:2. Each cell is a 20x40 logical pixel field. Use very large, crisp individual square pixels and real transparent background. THIS IS A SPARSE POISON STATUS VFX, NOT A CHARACTER OR SLIME COLUMN.
In EVERY cell the middle 16 columns x=2..17, y=4..39 are COMPLETELY EMPTY TRANSPARENT for the invisible champion. Only TWO columns at the extreme LEFT and TWO at the extreme RIGHT may contain bubbles. Each side has only 3 separated tiny round yellow-green bubbles, each 1x1 or 2x2 pixels, with large vertical EMPTY GAPS between them. No stalks, no connecting slime, no columns, no fog mass, no droplet chains. Each bubble has one near-white yellow-green highlight square. Above the invisible head ONLY, in the top FOUR rows, a tiny swamp-purple wisp centered on x=9..10. Everything LEFT-RIGHT symmetric in every frame.
Six frames: side bubbles rise through y=32,22,12, then 29,19,9, then 26,16,6, then some pop into two specks, then new bubbles appear at bottom, then return toward first. Clearly six different frames with no cross-cell spill.
Colors ONLY #F6FFC8 #D2F55E #8ED13A #4C9A2C and purple #EBD2FF #B884EE #8048BE. No outlines, no glow/blur, no antialiasing, no text, no actor, no ground, no shadows. All background fully transparent. Tiny isolated bubbles AT THE EXTREME EDGES OF EACH CELL, NEVER closer to center. Maintain the empty 16x36 body slot.
