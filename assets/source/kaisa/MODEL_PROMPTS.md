## 规则（含之前的英雄学到的）

- **游戏尺寸**：头顶到鞋底约 36 格，抬起的翼舱再高出 2–5 格（翼舱尖到鞋底最多约 41 格；本包女英雄算头发约 40 格，46–48 格在游戏里太大）。真正的低分辨率像素画放大 8 倍：每个像素一个 8×8 的纯色方块，全部对齐同一个 8 px 网格，没有比一格小的东西，没有抗锯齿、模糊、柔光、半透明。
- **干净**：最多 24 种颜色；每种材质 2–3 个平涂色阶（头发深紫、皮肤、紧身衣浅钢蓝、甲壳紫 + 一个亮边色、金、洋红 + 粉色亮芯、眼睛淡紫）；大块纯色；不要抖动、渐变、噪点，一块颜色里不要夹单个杂色方块。
- **只有一圈描边**：整个剪影外面一圈 1 格宽的近黑描边；描边里面的边缘和褶皱用那种材质自己最暗的色阶，**不要再画第二圈黑**。
- **深色英雄要亮**（她几乎全是深紫）：甲壳最暗的那一阶也要是看得出的深紫，比描边明显亮；每块甲壳的上沿 / 外沿留一格更亮的紫色亮边；身上不要大块描边色；浅钢蓝紧身衣、金边、洋红光都要亮。
- **翼舱**（她的标志）：两片都要看得见、贴着背、不留缺口、不挡脸。近处的翼舱从近肩后面向左上方张开，2–3 片尖刃，金边；远处的翼舱从远肩上方往右上升起，尖端比头顶高 2–5 格；每片里面一只洋红椭圆光眼（约 3×5 格，中间一格粉色亮芯），金边框住。
- **手**：深紫爪手，爪尖 1 格，五指张开，掌心各一格洋红光；手要读得出是手（护腕 + 手背 + 爪），不要画成 1 格的黑棍。
- **脸**（最重要）：A 头（头顶到下巴）约 10 格、脸至少 8 格；B 按原版英雄的 Q 版比例，头约 12 格（站高 36 格的三分之一）、脸约 10 格。两只眼睛都看得见、一样大、在同一行：每只 2 格宽 2 格高（上行一格白色高光 + 一格深色瞳孔，下行淡紫色），上面各一行深色睫毛；两眼之间 2 格皮肤，近处眼睛（画面左边，她朝右）前面留一列脸颊；淡紫色**只用在眼睛上**（洋红光、粉色纹路都要明显更艳更粉）；细眉毛用头发最深的紫色，贴在眼睛上方、朝鼻子方向往下斜（凶一点）；虚空纹：额头正中一条 1×2 格粉紫色竖纹（发际线下面），近处脸颊外眼角下面一格粉紫色，都不碰眼睛；头发在眉毛上方，中间隔一行皮肤，两边垂下来框住脸但**绝不盖住眼睛**；一格深豆沙色的嘴在脸中线上、眼睛下面两行；脸颊、下巴不要别的深色格（之前的英雄出现过下巴两角的阴影连成歪嘴笑）。
- **两版**：A 照原图比例（只把头画大，脸才看得清）；B 按原版英雄的 Q 版比例（头占身高三分之一，身体和腿短一点），站姿、翼舱、颜色不变。
- **脚底线以下什么都不能有**（游戏在脚下画血条）：鞋底最低一行正好在第 99 行方块，下面一格都不能有。
- 3/4 正面朝右（照原图），看得到脸和胸口，不画背影。
- 身体在画布中间（照 `kaisa_size_guide.png` 的位置），近处翼舱伸向左边、远处翼舱升向右上，所有格子都在画布里。
- 背景透明；做不到就用纯绿 `#00FF00`，**不要用品红或紫色**（她的光是洋红色的，会被一起抠掉）；不要网格线、文字、边框、影子。
- 如果模型不肯画有名字的角色，就不提名字，只保留外观描述（下面的提示词里已经没有名字）。

```text
Five attached images. FIRST: the look of this character - copy her face, long hair, suit, armour, the two raised wing-pods, colors and her stance from it. SECOND: official heroes of the game Teamfight Manager 2 at 8x (every game pixel an 8x8 block) - match their pixel size, cleanliness and their way of drawing faces. THIRD: heroes of this game's pack at 8x - the quality to reach: crisp small details, no mush; the last one shows how a dark-armoured hero stays readable with lit edges. FIFTH: a straight shrink of the FIRST image to game size at 8x - use it ONLY to see what fits at this size and where; it is blurry and broken, do not copy its look.
Task: redraw the FIRST image as clean hand-made pixel art AT GAME SIZE: about 36 pixels from the top of her hair to the soles, with the raised wing-pods reaching 2-5 pixels higher (at most about 41 from the pod tips to the soles) - true low-resolution pixel art shown enlarged 8x: every pixel one crisp 8x8 square on a single 8-px grid, nothing smaller than one square, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow, no semi-transparency. Keep the big readable shapes of the FIRST image and drop its fine filigree.
The character: a fierce young woman in a living alien battle-suit, standing upright as in the FIRST image, legs apart, arms held a little out from her sides, the clawed fingers spread with one glowing MAGENTA square in each palm. Long, thick DARK PURPLE hair (deep aubergine, 2-3 shades, one lighter violet highlight) parted on one side, framing her face and falling behind her shoulders to the waist, one lock over her far shoulder. A sleek PALE STEEL-BLUE bodysuit (3 cool shades) on the chest, the belly, the upper arms and the inner thighs, with a small V of skin at the neck and a dark violet collar-ridge round it. DARK VIOLET armour plates (3 shades, the darkest a deep violet clearly lighter than the outline, plus a 1-square LIGHTER VIOLET rim along the top and outer edge of every plate): rounded shoulder plates, long forearm gauntlets ending in dark clawed hands (claws 1-square points), plates down the outer thighs and the shins, pointed knee guards, clawed boots with a small heel. Thin GOLD trim lines (1 square, 2 shades) along the edges of the shoulder plates, the thigh plates and the pods. Glowing MAGENTA slits (hot magenta with a light-pink centre square) on the outer thighs and the forearms. Her signature: TWO LARGE WING-PODS raised behind her shoulders, half open like swept-back wings: the NEAR pod behind her near shoulder sweeps up and out to the LEFT as 2-3 pointed dark blades with a gold rim, the FAR pod rises over her far shoulder to the upper RIGHT, its tip 2-5 squares above the top of her hair; each pod shows one glowing MAGENTA OVAL EYE (about 3x5 squares, a lighter pink core square) inside a gold rim. Both pods join her back with no gap and never cover her face.
Pixel rules (most important): at most 24 colors in total, every material 2-3 flat shades (hair purple, skin, suit steel-blue, armour violet + its rim highlight, gold, magenta + pink core, eye lilac); big solid areas; no dithering, no gradients, no noise, no lone square of a different color inside an area. ONE 1-square near-black outline around the whole silhouette only: inside it, edges and folds use the material's own darkest shade - never a second ring of black inside the outline. Because she is dark, keep the rims lit: no large area of the outline color inside her, the armour reads as violet, the pale suit, the gold and the magenta stay bright.
Face (most important detail): A: the FIRST image's proportions, only the head bigger than in the FIRST image so the face reads: the head (top of the hair to the chin) about 10 squares and the face (hairline to chin) at least 8 squares tall; both eyes visible, the same size and on the same rows: each eye 2 squares wide and 2 tall - top row a white highlight square and a dark pupil square, bottom row LILAC (pale violet) - with a dark lash row over each eye; 2 squares of skin between the eyes and one cheek column before the near eye (left, she faces right); this LILAC is used NOWHERE else (the glows are a much hotter magenta-pink); thin brows in the hair's darkest purple just above the eyes, sloping down toward the nose (a fierce look); her void marking: one 1x2-square PINK-VIOLET stripe in the middle of the forehead under the hairline and one pink-violet square on the near cheek below the outer corner of the near eye, never touching an eye; the hair stays ABOVE the brows with one row of skin between and frames the cheeks without covering an eye; one dark mauve mouth square on the face's middle line two rows under the eyes; no other dark squares on the cheeks or the jaw (a shadow joining the jaw corners to the chin reads as a grin).
Pose and place: 3/4 FRONT view facing right as the FIRST image, face and chest visible, never her back. The soles on block row 99 (y 792-799) - the same ground as the FOURTH image - and NOTHING on or below row 100 (the game draws the health bar under the soles). Horizontally as the FIFTH image: her body in the middle, the near pod reaching out to the left and the far pod rising to the upper right; keep every square inside the canvas.
Layout: one 1024x1024 image per version (a 128x128-square canvas at 8x). Transparent background; if transparency is not possible use solid pure green #00FF00 - NEVER magenta or purple (her glows are magenta). No grid lines, no text, no border, no shadow.
Before finishing, check: about 36 squares from the top of the hair to the soles and the pods 2-5 higher; every square 8x8 on one grid; at most 24 colors; both eyes 2x2, level and visible, the lilac only in the eyes; both pods visible with their magenta eyes and joined to her back; the outline one square wide everywhere with no black ring inside it; the armour violet with lit rims, not black; nothing below the soles.
```
